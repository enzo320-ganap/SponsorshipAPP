GANAP Performing Arts Association Inc. (GANAP), the 2019 Manuel L. Quezon Gawad Parangal Awardee for Most Outstanding Institution, is a non-profit organization dedicated to cultivating the talents of underprivileged youth, molding them to become ambassadors of culture and the arts. The National Youth Commission accredits us as a youth-serving organization, and the Quezon City Council recognizes us as a QC Civil Society Organization.

From borrowed classrooms to makeshift rehearsal spaces, GANAP began as a nomadic ensemble of gifted youth from impoverished families—carried only by faith, music, and the kindness of those who believed in our mission. Over the years, we were lovingly adopted by institutions like Quezon City University, Erehwon Center for the Arts, Music Classroom, and even Filipino-American families in the U.S. Now, through unwavering prayers (PANATA), hard work, and the grace of our patrons, GANAP has built its first permanent sanctuary: GANAP Studios—a 90sqm performing arts center in Quezon City with a dance studio, recital hall, recording studio, and multimedia space. It is a home consecrated for the underserved youth who dare to dream.

On November 23, 2025, we celebrate this milestone through HAYAG: Mga Awit ng Panalangin, Paninindigan, Pag-asa, Pag-ibig at Pasasalamat—a concert of sacred, gospel, and Filipino songs that reflect our journey of faith, resilience, and purpose.

PROFILE

GANAP (Galaw, Awit, Ngiti, Arte at Palakpak) Performing Arts Association, Inc. is a

non-profit organization established on 01 September 2016, originally known as GANAP Quezon City Polytechnic University. It was officially registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission on 28 May 2018, and accredited by the National Youth Commission on 06 November 2023 as a youth-serving organization. GANAP is also recognized by the Quezon City Government as a Civil Society Organization, envisioned as a creative sanctuary for impoverished but gifted youth, molded to become ambassadors of culture and the arts, bringing honor and glory to the country.

On its first international tour in 2018, GANAP earned three silver diplomas at the 5th Singapore International Choral Festival, was selected as one of only 14 international choirs at the 3rd Macau International Choir Festival, and was invited by the Holy See as the Featured Performer during the birthday concert in honor of His Holiness Pope Francis.

In 2019, GANAP received the prestigious Manuel L. Quezon Gawad Parangal for Most Outstanding Institution, then embarked on its USA Goodwill Concert Tour, where it was honored with three Special Congressional Recognitions from the US House of Representatives and featured on Good Morning Las Vegas via KNTV Channel 13.

GANAP has since blossomed into a multi-disciplinary performing ensemble—a theatre company, dance troupe, band, and choir in one—renowned for its original musicales

that reflect the political and socio-cultural narratives of the Filipino people, and for its world-class concerts that celebrate resilience, identity, and hope. Even during the pandemic, GANAP remained active through outreach efforts and recently completed its 5th International Goodwill Concert Tour in the USA.

Under the visionary leadership of Dr. Alexis Jan “Agoy” Patacsil, the unrelenting dedication of its members, and the steadfast support of its patrons, GANAP has transformed from a nomadic collective—rehearsing in borrowed spaces and adopted homes—into a rooted institution with a permanent sanctuary: GANAP Studios.

Located in Quezon City, GANAP Studios is the first-ever self-funded performing arts center built by a Filipino non-profit association for underserved youth. Spanning 90 square meters, it houses a dance and recital studio, a recording studio, and a multimedia studio—a consecrated space where dreams are nurtured, talents are refined, and the mission of uplifting vulnerable sectors through the arts continues with renewed strength and purpose.
